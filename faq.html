Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about becoming a Virtual Assistant

❓ Who can apply to become a Virtual Assistant?

Anyone with good English communication skills, basic computer literacy, and reliable internet access from any country can apply. No prior experience is required as we provide training for beginners!

📝 How do I apply for the Virtual Assistant position?

Simply click the "Apply Now" button on our website, fill out the application form with your details, skills, and experience. Our team will review your application and contact you within 3-5 business days.

💰 How does the commission structure work?

You earn between 5% to 10% commission on every successful sale you make. The rate depends on your performance:

  • Entry Level: 5% commission
  • Mid Level: 7% commission
  • Top Performer: 10% commission

The more you sell, the higher your commission rate!

💵 When and how do I get paid?

Payments are processed monthly via:

  • Bank transfer
  • Mobile money (Airtel, MTN, etc.)
  • PayPal

You'll receive detailed statements of all your sales and commissions.

🏆 How do I get the Certificate of Excellence?

You will receive an official SEEDWEL Certificate of Excellence after successfully referring 10 clients. This certificate recognizes your dedication and achievement as a valued member of Seedwel Investment Limited.

🎁 What holiday bonuses are available?

Top-performing Virtual Assistants receive generous bonuses during:

  • Christmas & New Year
  • Easter
  • Independence Day (Zambia)
  • Other major holidays

Bonuses are based on your performance and sales volume.

📈 Is there room for career growth?

Yes! We have a clear career growth path:

  • Trainee VA → Certified VA → Senior VA → Team Lead → Manager

Promotions come with higher commission rates and additional benefits.

🌍 Can I work from anywhere?

Absolutely! This is a 100% remote position. You can work from home, a coffee shop, or anywhere with a stable internet connection. All you need is a laptop/computer and good internet.

⏰ What are the working hours?

We offer flexible working hours! You can choose:

  • Full-time (40+ hours/week)
  • Part-time (20-30 hours/week)
  • Flexible schedule based on your availability

You manage your own time as long as you meet your targets.

🛠️ What skills do I need?

We're looking for candidates with any of these skills:

  • Customer Service & Communication
  • Data Entry & Administrative Support
  • Social Media Management
  • Email Management
  • Sales & Marketing
  • Basic computer literacy

Training is provided for motivated beginners!

📊 How do I track my sales and earnings?

After being hired, you'll get access to your personal Worker Dashboard where you can:

  • Track all your sales and commissions
  • Submit daily reports
  • View tasks assigned by admin
  • Receive notifications and messages
